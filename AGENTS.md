# Architecture rules

- The first route is the operational evidence workspace, not a marketing page, because the product is designed for investigation work.
- Present model-generated conclusions as assessments and keep them visually distinct from directly measured file metadata and hashes.
- Preserve existing analysis modules behind evidence-oriented navigation until their underlying engines are independently replaced or validated.
- Apply the shared assessment policy to both fresh and cached model outputs; general-purpose visual reviews cannot provide calibrated authenticity probabilities or fabricated measurements.
- Keep legacy analysis views available in source but display not-performed states until independent engines are validated, so simulated outputs cannot masquerade as forensic evidence.
